UK immigration

Most of our work is immigration. We prepare and submit applications, explain what the Home Office will look for, and take on refusals, appeals and reviews. You get a fixed fee before we start.

We can help with

  • Spouse, partner, child and family visas
  • Skilled Worker visas and sponsor licences for employers
  • Student, Child Student and visitor visas
  • Global Talent and business routes
  • Indefinite leave to remain and British citizenship
  • Long residence, 7-year child and human rights applications
  • Appeals, administrative review and judicial review
  • Immigration bail and civil penalties for illegal working

Residential property

We handle the conveyancing when you buy or sell a home in England and Wales, from searches and contracts to completion and registration. We explain each document in Chinese if you prefer.

We can help with

  • Buying and selling freehold and leasehold property
  • Searches, title checks and contract review
  • Working with your mortgage lender
  • Completion and Land Registry registration
